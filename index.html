Biography

Shenzhen University I am currently a graduate student in the School of Computer Science and Software Engineering at Shenzhen University. I am very fortunate to be studying and conducting research in fields such as edge embodied intelligence, generative diffusion and VLA models, and edge/micro-machines under the guidance of Professor Qihua Zhou.

I earned my bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from the School of Artificial Intelligence at Jiangxi Normal University.In Professor Jianmao Xiao ’s lab, I participated in the implementation and deployment of the “AnjuYinchiao” project—a spin-off of the Fangyuan Tianlun initiative—focusing on research related to the Internet of Things (IoT) and edge computing. At the same time, I gained in-depth knowledge of technologies such as NLP, RAG, and KG while contributing to materials science research within the AI4M initiative.

I hope that in the near future, through my own efforts, I can make a meaningful contribution to the development of optimization strategies for edge devices with limited resources.

Research Experience

MatSCI–Intelligent Research Assistance Platform for Chemical Materials Science 2025.01 – 2026.06
Deputy Head NLP · RAG · FastAPI

Focusing on the interdisciplinary field of natural language processing and chemical materials science, we are building a closed-loop intelligent framework centered on “knowledge—prediction—validation” and developing three core capabilities: intelligent document analysis, structured information extraction, and materials optimization design.


Personal Responsibility：Led the end-to-end development of the system; collaborated with team members to train and fine-tune NLP models and RAG algorithms using the AutoDL cloud platform; used FastAPI to build model service APIs; developed front-end and back-end data interaction logic; formulated a development plan; and coordinated three team members to advance front-end and back-end development, model training, and testing according to milestones.

Prism Stage—A New Perspective on AI Dance 2024.05 – 2025.06
Patent Authorized (Co-author) Knowledge Graph · RAG · Motion Capture

Intelligent dance teaching assistance software, with MIDCAT+DAGK knowledge graph as the core, combined with RAG+LLM to generate personalized plans, creating a "virtual instructor + motion library + AI choreography" platform.


Personal Responsibility: Optimized dance movement node capture algorithm (based on MIDCAT technology, improving capture accuracy of 71 joints), designed and built a single-dance-type dance movement knowledge graph (DAKG), combined RAG with the knowledge base to establish movement evaluation feedback mechanism, and collaborated with LLM to optimize user interaction experience.
